To resolve this, you need to remove the archived Jira project from the integration settings and then click Save and Activate.

A similar behavior applies to Requirement Integration. If a requirement from an archived project was imported into qTest, removing that project from the integration settings will convert the imported requirements into internal qTest requirements.
